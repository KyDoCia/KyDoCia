## About

I am a Roblox developer with more than seven years of experience working with Roblox Studio and Luau. My work is centered around complex gameplay systems, multiplayer architecture, secure client-server communication, maintainable code and production-focused optimization.

I am currently expanding my workflow into RobloxTS, TypeScript, Rojo and modern collaborative development practices to contribute to larger international productions.

I approach each system as part of a complete product: architecture, security, performance, usability, scalability and long-term maintainability all matter.

## Current Portfolio Project

### Advanced Roleplay Vehicle & Dealership Ecosystem

A large-scale portfolio project designed to demonstrate interconnected production systems rather than isolated scripts.

**Planned and developing systems include:**

- Dealership purchasing and vehicle ownership
- Persistent garages and vehicle retrieval
- Vehicle keys and access permissions
- Registration plates and ownership records
- Police radar, fines and enforcement workflows
- Impound yard and vehicle seizure systems
- Police tablet and operational tools
- Banking and economy integration
- Fuel, damage and maintenance systems
- Secure networking and server-side validation
- Modular configuration and extensibility
- Cross-platform and performance-conscious design

The public repository, technical documentation, architecture diagrams and playable demonstration will be added as development progresses.
